About

Vice City Stream Clips is a fan-run clip channel. A small personal tool finds popular moments from public GTA VI streams, edits them into 9:16 shorts with subtitles, and publishes them to our own accounts on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube through each platform's official API.

Every clip names the streamer it comes from. We are not affiliated with Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive or any streamer featured in the clips. Grand Theft Auto is a trademark of its respective owner.

Streamers and rights holders

If you are a streamer or rights holder and want a clip removed, or want your channel excluded, send a direct message to @vicecitystreamclips on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube. We remove the clip and exclude the channel promptly.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy covers the Vice City Stream Clips publishing tool ("the app") and the accounts @vicecitystreamclips on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by the account owner to publish videos to their own accounts. It does not offer sign-up to the public and has no other users.

What data the app processes

  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, Instagram (Meta) and Google/YouTube when the account owner signs in. They are used only to upload videos and read basic account and video statistics for the owner's own accounts.
  • Basic profile information of the owner's own accounts (account ID and username), used to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Statistics of our own published videos (views, likes, comments counts), used to decide which kinds of clips to make.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data about viewers, followers or other users.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, encrypted in the Windows Credential Manager. No server or cloud database is involved. Access can be revoked at any time in the security settings of TikTok, Instagram/Facebook or the Google account.

Contact

Questions about this policy: direct message to @vicecitystreamclips on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube.
